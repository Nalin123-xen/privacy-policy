Privacy Policy for VTPT – TC Inspection

Draft for organizational review — do not publish until the retention and deletion section is completed and all practices are confirmed.

Effective date: 23 November 2026

App and responsible organization

This Privacy Policy applies to VTPT – TC Inspection, an application provided by Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation (TNSDC). The Android package identifier is com.tnsdc.assessor_app. Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation is the developer identified in the app’s Google Play listing.

For privacy questions and requests, contact support@tnsdc.in.

Purpose and users

VTPT – TC Inspection supports authorized officials and evaluators carrying out training-centre inspections, course and batch verification, assessments, and related monitoring and reporting activities.

Information processed

Depending on the authorized workflow, the app processes:

  • Account and profile information used to authenticate users and identify their official role and assignments.
  • Training-centre, course, batch, trainer, assessment and inspection records, including verification results, ratings, remarks and submitted supporting information.
  • Photographs and documents selected or captured for inspection evidence. Evidence can contain information about trainers, candidates or other individuals recorded during an inspection.
  • Device location, including latitude and longitude, for location-based inspection functions, maps, and photograph watermarks. Watermarks can also include the date and time.
  • Technical diagnostics, including app version, device and operating-system information, error details, crash reports and supported historical Android process-exit information, to investigate failures and improve reliability.

How information is used

Information is used to provide authorized access, display assignments, conduct and verify inspections, capture and submit evidence, maintain inspection and assessment records, generate reports, support monitoring, and diagnose technical problems.

Permissions and local storage

Camera access is used to capture inspection photographs. Location permission is used for location-based features and photograph watermarks. The Android app does not declare background-location permission.

The app can store authentication/session information, drafts, captured images and cached files on the device. Declining a required permission can prevent the corresponding feature from working. Permissions can be changed in the device settings.

Data transmission and service providers

Submitted inspection information and evidence are sent to the application’s backend services for the relevant official workflow. Firebase Crashlytics processes diagnostic information, and Firebase Remote Config supplies application configuration.

Map and route functions use external map-tile and routing services. Requests can disclose the requested map area or route coordinates and network information to those services. Opening a document with an external Office application or browser makes it subject to that application's or website's privacy practices.

Organizational review required: confirm the backend operator, authorized recipients, service providers and any additional disclosure obligations before publication. The mobile source alone does not establish backend access or sharing practices.

Retention and deletion

Complete before publishing: Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation must specify how long account information, submitted inspection records, photographs, documents and diagnostic information are retained; the criteria used to determine retention; how users request deletion; and which records must be retained for official, audit or legal requirements.

Privacy and deletion inquiries may be sent to support@tnsdc.in. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not itself delete records already submitted to backend services.

Security

The app uses authentication and platform storage mechanisms to support authorized access and local session storage. Data protection depends on both the mobile application and the backend services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Organizational review required: verify actual transport encryption, backend storage safeguards and access controls before making additional security commitments.

Policy changes

Changes to the app’s data practices should be reflected in this policy and its effective date. The current policy should remain accessible through the app and its Google Play listing.
