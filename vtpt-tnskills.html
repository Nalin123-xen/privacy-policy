Privacy Policy

VTPT - TN Skills

TNSDC (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the VTPT - TN Skills mobile application (“App”). This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared, and handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on your role and the features you use. It may include:

  • Account and profile information: name, mobile number, email address, date of birth, gender, address, and account identifiers.
  • Education and employment information: institution, qualifications, skills, work experience, resume, and information submitted during student or trainer registration.
  • Identity information: Aadhaar number and verification status where requested as part of registration or verification.
  • Learning and assessment information: course enrollments, learning records, psychometric assessment responses, and results.
  • Applications and attendance: job, internship, and job-fair participation records, attendance submissions, and related verification information.
  • Photos and documents: images, attendance photos, resumes, and other files you submit.
  • Location information: your device’s location when you use features that verify job-fair attendance or add location details to attendance photos.
  • Technical information: device and operating-system details, app version, installation identifiers, crash reports, and diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create accounts, authenticate users, and maintain profiles.
  • Process registration and eligibility verification.
  • Provide courses, assessments, and skill-development services.
  • Process employment, internship, and job-fair participation.
  • Record and verify attendance.
  • Provide support and service-related communications.
  • Diagnose errors, improve reliability, and configure the App.
  • Prevent misuse and meet applicable administrative or legal requirements.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan job-fair QR codes and capture photos for attendance or other supported submissions.
  • Location: used for attendance verification and location details on attendance photos.
  • Photos and files: used when you choose an image or document to upload.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores sign-in tokens and account-related preferences locally to maintain your session. Logging out clears locally stored account preferences. Logging out or uninstalling the App does not, by itself, delete records held on our servers.

5. Firebase Services

The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to retrieve application configuration and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and errors. These services may process installation identifiers, device and app information, and diagnostic data. Crash reports may be associated with your account identifier and user role to help investigate issues.

Learn more in Firebase’s privacy and security information .

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with authorised programme administrators, educational institutions, training providers, employers, or job-fair organisers where needed to deliver a service you use or process an application you submit.

Service providers supporting hosting, application configuration, diagnostics, or other operations may process information for those purposes. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid request from an authorised authority.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for the periods needed to provide services and meet programme, administrative, audit, and legal requirements.

[Insert the organisation’s approved retention periods or criteria, including treatment of inactive accounts, attendance records, assessment records, uploaded documents, and backups.]

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and verification codes confidential.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You can update information through the profile features available in the App and manage device permissions through your device settings. To request access, correction, account deletion, or deletion of personal information, contact us using the details below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some records may need to be retained to meet applicable legal or programme requirements; we will explain any such restrictions.

10. Users Under 18

Some educational programmes may involve users under 18. Where required, participation and processing of their personal information must follow the applicable parent, guardian, or institution authorisation process. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been submitted without the required authorisation.

11. External Services

The App may open external websites, learning platforms, or recruitment services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. Review those policies before submitting information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App or our practices change. The revised policy will show an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.
